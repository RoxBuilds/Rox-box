# Code of conduct

Rox-box is a small, non-commercial, volunteer project. These rules apply
everywhere the project meets people: issues, pull requests, discussions, the
Discord server and any other space run in its name.

## How we treat each other

- Be kind and patient. Most players are not engineers, and many contributors
  are learning. Explain; do not mock.
- Argue about the work, not the person. Disagree with a design, a patch or a
  result as hard as you like, but never attack someone's character, background
  or skill.
- No harassment, threats, hate, slurs, sexual or violent content, doxxing,
  or sharing anyone's private information.
- Keep it on topic and keep it honest. Say what you know and what you do not.
  Do not demand fixes: nobody here is paid, and nobody owes you a title.
- Newcomers get a fair hearing. A clearly described problem is welcome even if
  it turns out to be a duplicate or a mistake.

## What we do not allow, because it endangers the project

Rox-box exists only because nothing proprietary is ever part of it. So, in any
project space:

- Do not share, offer, ask for or link to game files, dumps, keys,
  certificates, cracks, leaked or proprietary SDK material, or any other
  copyrighted material you have no right to distribute. That includes hints
  about where to get them.
- Do not name a specific title or publisher in an issue, pull request or
  commit, and do not post logs, screenshots or video made from running a title
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Do not ask for support in using Rox-box with files you did not obtain
  legally.

Content that breaks these rules is **removed**, without a debate, and may be
edited or hidden from history. Repeating it gets you removed from the space.

## Who decides, and what happens

The maintainers moderate. They may edit or remove comments, close or lock
threads, and warn, mute, temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks these
rules, taking into account how serious the matter is and whether it was
intended. Their decision is final, but you may ask for it to be looked at again
by writing to a maintainer privately.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these rules, report it to a maintainer in a private message
on Discord, or through the repository's private security reporting form
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) if it is serious. Reports are treated
confidentially, and nobody is punished for reporting in good faith.
